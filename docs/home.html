How it works

On a schedule you own, in your timezone.

01

Pull

Connects to each mailbox over read-only IMAP. It never marks a message read.

02

Window

Keeps only what arrived since the last run — the window is the dedupe.

03

Triage

One model call, with your brief and your rules, decides what you actually see.

04

Send

One short email with the replies already drafted — or nothing, if nothing mattered.

Private by construction.

◇

No server, anywhere

It runs on your GitHub Actions with your own AI credentials. Fork the repo and it becomes entirely yours — no analytics, no telemetry, no hosted anything, and no account with anyone.

◈

Read-only where it counts

The INBOX connection is opened read-only, so mailtriage can never mark a message read. It appends drafts and applies labels; it never sends, and never deletes.

◆

Fewer, never padded

The item count is a ceiling, not a target. On a quiet window the email is short — and if nothing cleared the bar, no email sends at all.
